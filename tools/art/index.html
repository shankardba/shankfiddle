Six ways to cross-breed spirographs, fractals, L-systems, and Voronoi cells into one image — warp a curve through a Mandelbrot set, trap it in fractal orbit bands, crossfade it into a branching rosette, or ring it into a mirrored mandala. Pan, zoom, randomize, and watch the live color-coded equations update as you go.
